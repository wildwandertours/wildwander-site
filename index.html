Prep for the trip

Practice your Spanish.

Weekly video calls to sharpen real conversational Spanish — for travellers who already have some Spanish and want to build the confidence to actually speak it in Colombia.

Hear it from Riley

"I want to speak Spanish with you."

What it is

Conversation practice,
not a classroom.

This is for travellers who already have a working base in Spanish — from school, an app, or past trips — and want to actually use it. Relaxed, one-on-one video calls focused on real spoken Spanish: ordering food, asking for directions, chatting with your driver, haggling at a market. Not a beginner course, not grammar from scratch — just practice, with someone fluent who knows exactly what comes up on a trip through Colombia.

Why it's worth it

01

Real travel Spanish

Colloquial Colombian slang and local expressions — the words guides, hosts, and locals actually use, not textbook dialogue you'll never hear.

02

Low-pressure practice

No classroom, no grading — just real conversation, sharpening the Spanish you already have.

03

From someone who lives it

Practice with someone fluent, speaking Spanish day to day while living in Colombia.

04

Built around your trip

Sessions timed to your departure and focused on what you'll actually need for your itinerary.

About Wild Wander Tours

Best friends who
couldn't stay away.

The story

How it began

Madison Gorsh and Riley Ferré are two best friends who met through their love of paragliding. Both spent years in Colombia, falling for the vibrant culture, the abundant nature, and the generous people. In 2025, that passion became Wild Wander Tours.

Our mission

Safe, interactive tours built on cross-cultural exchange, respect for nature, and the kind of adventure you only find by immersing yourself in a new place.

Your guides
